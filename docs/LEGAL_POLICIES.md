# YukCep Legal Policy Outline

## KVKK / Privacy
- Purpose limitation: user/account, listing, bid and notification operations.
- Data classes: identity, contact, role, listing records, runtime security logs.
- Retention: minimum operational period + legal obligation windows.
- User rights: access, correction, deletion, objection, portability.

## Terms of Use
- Platform is a marketplace; shipment contract is between parties.
- Users must provide accurate listing and bid information.
- Fraudulent, abusive or illegal content is prohibited.
- YukCep may suspend accounts that violate policies.

## Cookie Policy
- Essential cookies: auth/session continuity.
- Functional cookies: user preferences.
- Analytics cookies: product performance insights (opt-in where required).

## Enforcement
- Abuse report intake (`abuse_reports`) + moderation queue.
- Repeat abuse can trigger listing removal and account restrictions.
